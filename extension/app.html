Data & privacy

Backstory reads the GitHub URL, page title, and selected text only after you click Capture. It does not scan your browsing history or repositories in the background.

Cards and unfinished captures stay in this Chrome profile’s local extension storage. Nothing is sent to an AI service. Backstory does not encrypt this data; someone with access to your profile or device may be able to read it.

Do not save credentials or code you are not allowed to retain. Exports contain plain-text notes and snippets. Review them before sharing. Export a backup before uninstalling: uninstalling removes local extension data.

Missing-detail checks only look for filled fields. They do not verify facts, authorship, results, or links. Practice questions use fixed rules, not AI.

The optional Gemini adapter is disabled and is not part of the extension runtime.
