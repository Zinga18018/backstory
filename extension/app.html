YOUR WORK, IN YOUR WORDS

A small collection of decisions worth remembering.

Private by design. Not a vault.

Backstory reads the GitHub URL, page title, and selected text only after you click Capture. It never scans your browsing history or reads whole repositories in the background.

Cards and unfinished captures live in this Chrome profile’s local extension storage. Nothing is sent to an AI service. Local storage is not encrypted by Backstory; someone with access to your profile or device may be able to read it.

Do not save credentials or confidential code you are not allowed to keep. Export files contain your notes and snippets as plain text. Check them before sharing. Uninstalling removes local extension data; keep a backup if you need it.

Missing-detail checks look for filled fields. They do not verify facts, authorship, results, or whether a link proves your claim. Practice questions are fixed rules, not AI output.

The optional Gemini adapter is a disabled development stub and is not included in the extension runtime.
